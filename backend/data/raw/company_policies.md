# Company Policies

## Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Orders placed after 3 PM EST will be processed the next business day. During peak seasons (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Holiday Season), processing may take up to 3 business days.

## Price Matching

We offer price matching within 14 days of purchase. If you find the same item at a lower price from an authorized retailer, contact support with proof and we will refund the difference. Price matching does not apply to marketplace sellers, auction sites, or clearance items.

## Gift Cards

Gift cards are available in denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $250. They never expire and can be used for any product on our website. Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash. Lost or stolen gift cards cannot be replaced.

## Loyalty Program

Our rewards program earns 1 point per dollar spent. Accumulate 100 points to receive a $5 reward. Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity. Silver members (500+ points/year) get free express shipping. Gold members (1000+ points/year) get early access to sales and exclusive discounts.

## Privacy Policy Summary

We collect only the data necessary to process your orders and improve your experience. We never sell personal information to third parties. You can request a copy of your data or deletion of your account at any time through Account Settings.
